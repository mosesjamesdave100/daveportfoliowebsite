FAQ

Questions creators ask before we start

Straight answers, no sales talk. If yours isn't here, use the chat bubble in the corner or send it through the contact form.

What services do you offer?

YouTube Automation, YouTube Marketing, Video Editing, YouTube SEO, Channel Optimization, Twitch Marketing, Music Promotion, and Merch/Website Design. You can bundle a few together if your channel needs more than one thing fixed at once.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your channel's size and what you actually need, so I don't post a flat price list. Fill out the contact form with a bit about your channel and I'll send a real quote, not a generic package.

How long does it take to see results?

It varies. Some channels see movement within a few weeks, others take a couple of months, especially if we're fixing years of inconsistent uploads. I'll give you an honest timeline after the first audit, not a guess to make the pitch sound better.

Do I need to give you access to my channel?

Depends on the service. Full channel management or automation needs manager-level access. SEO reviews, strategy, and consulting can usually be done over a call or screen-share, with you making the changes yourself if you'd rather keep full control.

Is the music promotion real listeners, or bots?

Real listeners through legitimate placement and targeting, not purchased plays. That's why the growth in the Results section climbs steadily instead of spiking overnight and disappearing a week later.

Can you guarantee monetization or a certain number of subscribers?

No one can honestly guarantee platform approval or exact numbers, and I won't pretend otherwise. What I can guarantee is a real plan built on what's worked for other channels, adjusted as your channel's numbers come in.

What if my channel is brand new with zero subscribers?

That's fine. I work with channels at every stage, from zero subscribers to a few hundred thousand. Newer channels often move faster because there's less to undo first.

How fast do you reply?

Within 1 hour during active hours. If you message outside of that, you'll still hear back the same day in almost every case.
